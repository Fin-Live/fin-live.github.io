What to do with the CSV

The database stores the codes but knows nothing about who holds them. Put the downloaded codes next to your class list in your own spreadsheet, mail-merge one code to each student, and keep that spreadsheet off this site. That mapping is what turns the exported responses back into named participation, and it is the one thing that should never leave your own machine.
